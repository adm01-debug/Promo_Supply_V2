### Why this shape
- The generator is the single source of truth — adding locales anywhere in `src/` automatically expands coverage on the next CI run, no manual sync.
- The new assertions exercise locale-specific data paths (`localize`, `formatLong`), so a malformed locale bundle (e.g. wrong subpath casing like `pt-br` vs `pt-BR`) fails loudly instead of silently passing the regex check.
- The "distinct weekday" cross-check is cheap insurance against import-resolution bugs once a second locale lands.
